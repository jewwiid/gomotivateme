# GoMotivateMe content formats

How to turn the generic ad-format list into posts for this product. Companion to [ad-formats.md](ad-formats.md). Voice and claims follow [story-first-llm-media-guide.md](story-first-llm-media-guide.md). Carousel layout follows [carousel-production-guide.md](carousel-production-guide.md).

**The product in one line.** A free public page for a goal: share one link, post progress, let chosen people react.

**The hero.** The person with the goal. GoMotivateMe is the guide. Isolation is the problem, not other apps and not the user’s character.

**One CTA per piece.** Use one of: Start your page · Share your goal · Invite your people · Who would you want in your corner?

Do not promise they will finish the goal. Do not use productivity-shame, fake scarcity, fake reviews, or competitor dunks.

## Verdict key

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ship** | Make this now with current assets |
| **Adapt** | Same wrapper, rewritten so it stays on-brand |
| **Wait** | Needs a live goal, a real quote, or a real number first |
| **Skip** | Off-brand or fake. Use the replacement named in the row |

## Platforms

Lead on **X**. Reddit is for native posts, not carousels. Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn get repurposed statics, carousels, and one short video a week until X is a habit.

| Platform | Static | Carousel | Video | Native extra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **X** | 1:1 or 16:9 still. Goal OG card counts. | 1:1, 2–6 images | 15–45s screen-record or captions-on-still. 9:16 also works. | Threads = long form. Quote-tweet your own goal updates. |
| **Instagram Feed** | 1:1 or 4:5 | 4:5, 5–7 slides | Skip; use Reels | Caption does the story; first slide does the hook |
| **Instagram Stories / Reels** | 9:16 still (art in lower half) | Not native; stitch slides as a Reel | 9:16, 15–30s, captions on screen | Poll / question sticker: “Who’s in your corner?” |
| **TikTok** | Weak; treat as video cover | Weak | 9:16, 15–30s, hook in 1s | Series: Day N of the founder’s real goal |
| **LinkedIn** | 1:1 | 1:1, 5–6 slides | 30–90s talking-head or screen-record | Founder-as-guide origin, not founder-as-hero |
| **Reddit** | Screenshot of a real goal or Notes | Do not post Canva carousels | Rare; link a Loom in comments | Write the post as a person with a goal. Mention the tool once. |
| **Email** | Header from the Canva pack | No | No | Same story, consent-only marketing |

## Media specs

### Static image

- Canvas: **1080 × 1080** (X, LinkedIn) or **1080 × 1350** (IG). Stories/Reels stills: **1080 × 1920**, ivory `#FBFAF6`, artwork in the lower half.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans SemiBold for the hook, Regular for one supporting line. Cobalt `#2856C7` on ivory. Yellow `#FEB704` only as the sun already in the art, or a 2px underline.
- One illustration from `marketing/canva-social-pack/` or a product screenshot. Do not collage art + UI + stickers.
- Wordmark: final frame of a carousel or unused on a single still. Never on the artwork itself.
- Hook: 8–14 words. Supporting line: 1–2 lines. No paragraph on the image.

### Carousel

Use the locked Canva template in the production guide.

| Slide | Job |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook the tension |
| 2 | Recognition |
| 3 | Reframe |
| 4 | One product step |
| 5 | What support looks like |
| 6 | One CTA |

One idea per slide, one approved PNG, folio `01 / 06`. Caption extends the story; it does not repeat slide 1.

### Video

Month one: **no face required.** Screen-record the product or Ken Burns the stills with on-screen captions.

| Length | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 8–15s | Hook + one line + CTA. Reels/TikTok/X |
| 20–40s | Create a goal, post a check-in, show a reaction |
| 45–90s | LinkedIn / X long form / Product Hunt gallery |

Rules:

- First sentence on screen in the first second. No logo sting.
- Captions always. Many people watch muted.
- End card: ivory, one CTA, wordmark small.
- Record 1080 × 1920 for Reels/TikTok; crop a 1:1 centre cut for X if needed.
- Audio: quiet, or none. Do not chase trending sounds that fight the tone.

## The 46 formats for this product

### 1. AI podcast — Skip

Fake podcasts selling a goal app will sound like an ad. Replacement: **native** founder voice note or a real conversation with a supporter, if you ever record one.

| Media | Do not make |
| --- | --- |
| Static / carousel / video | Two AI hosts “discovering” GoMotivateMe |

### 2. Breaking news — Adapt

Only for a true launch or a true change. Never fake a ticker.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Ivory card: “Now live: public goal pages with reactions.” No red siren. |
| **Carousel** | Slide 1 “What’s new” → what it is → who it’s for → how to start → CTA |
| **Video** | 15s screen-record of the new thing, captions only |
| **Platforms** | X, LinkedIn, IG. Not Reddit. |

### 3. Bundle offer — Skip

There is nothing to bundle. Replacement: **problem vs solution** (page + invite + check-in as one plan, not three paid SKUs).

### 4. Case of emergency — Skip

Goals are not an emergency. Replacement: **X signs** or **problems**.

### 5. Claymation — Skip

Wrong production cost and wrong texture. The journey illustrations already interrupt the feed. Replacement: Ken Burns on a Canva PNG (**doodle** / static).

### 6. Crossed-out problems — Ship

Strike through isolation problems the product actually changes. Leave one line uncrossed: the work is still work.

**Copy to use**

- ~~Nobody can see the effort~~
- ~~You have to re-explain the goal every time~~
- ~~A pause feels like disappearing~~
- The goal is still yours to do

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Four lines, three struck, `quiet-middle.png` |
| **Carousel** | One problem per slide, strikethrough on 2–5, CTA on 6 |
| **Video** | Same lines appearing, then a red-cobalt strike (use `#18201C`, not alarm red) |
| **Platforms** | IG carousel, LinkedIn, X images, Reels |

### 7. Customer testimonial — Wait

Use only a real person, named, with permission. Until then, quote **your own** check-in as the founder-user, labelled as you.

| Media | When you have a quote |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Quote + first name + `encouragement.png`. No fake 5-star row. |
| **Carousel** | Their situation → what they shared → what support felt like → CTA. Do not invent the after. |
| **Video** | Voice note or talking-head they recorded. Do not lip-sync. |
| **Platforms** | All, once real. |

### 8. Don't be an idiot — Skip

Shame is banned. Replacement: **myth vs fact** or **X signs**.

### 9. Don't buy this — Adapt

You are free. Flip it: “Don’t buy another tracker if what you need is a few people.” Do not name competitors.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | “Don’t buy more willpower. Invite two people.” `people-in-your-corner.png` |
| **Carousel** | What not to add (streaks, shame, money stakes) → what to do instead → CTA |
| **Video** | 12s caption-led. No roast montage. |
| **Platforms** | X, LinkedIn, IG |

### 10. Doodle — Adapt

Do not replace brand art with marker-pen ads. If you teach on camera, draw the three-step plan on paper *beside* the product, or animate captions over journey art.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | The three steps as type over `make-a-plan.png` |
| **Carousel** | One step per slide: create → share → check in |
| **Video** | Overhead of a notebook, then cut to the live page |
| **Platforms** | Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn |

### 11. Doctor — Skip

No clinical claims. Replacement: **stat headline** with Harkin 2016 cited, or **whiteboard**.

### 12. Email screenshot — Wait / Adapt

Only a real lifecycle email the recipient opted into, cropped, or a mock of *your* inbox asking a friend how the goal is going. Never a fake “your goal is failing” scare mail.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Crop of a real check-in email you received, or a plain-text “how’s the writing?” |
| **Carousel** | Weak. One still is enough. |
| **Video** | Scroll a real thread. |
| **Platforms** | X, LinkedIn |

### 13. Google search — Ship

Type a real query. The result is the product, described honestly.

**Queries to use:** `how to stay accountable without a coach` · `share a goal with friends` · `public goal tracker`

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Search bar + one result card for gomotivateme.com |
| **Carousel** | Related questions as slides, last slide is the page |
| **Video** | Type the query, click through to a real goal |
| **Platforms** | IG, TikTok, X, Reels |

### 14. Greenscreen — Adapt

You in front of a **real** goal page, a real Reddit thread you wrote, or a Notes screenshot. Not in front of a competitor ad.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | N/A |
| **Carousel** | N/A |
| **Video** | 20s: point at the quiet-middle update, say why you posted it |
| **Platforms** | TikTok, Reels. Optional later. Month 1 can skip face. |

### 15. Hack 101 — Adapt

Not a life hack. A small method: “Post the check-in even if the number is small.”

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | “Small check-in 101” + `small-check-in.png` |
| **Carousel** | 5 slides: what to write in an update (moved / hard / next) |
| **Video** | Screen-record writing that update |
| **Platforms** | IG carousel, X, Reels |

### 16. Instagram Story — Ship

Use the pack’s 9:16 layout. Poll sticker. One CTA.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Ivory 9:16, art lower half, hook upper half |
| **Carousel** | Sequence 3 Stories: tension → page → “reply with a goal you’d share” |
| **Video** | Same as Reels, 15s |
| **Platforms** | IG Stories, IG Reels cover |

### 17. iPhone Notes — Ship

Yellow Notes. One private thought. Leave it slightly unfinished.

**Notes to rotate**

- Day 11. Nobody knows I’m doing this. That’s the problem.
- I don’t need a crowd. I need two people who already care.
- Paused for a week. The page is still there. Returning counts.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Notes screenshot. Caption adds the link. |
| **Carousel** | Three notes, last one is the invite |
| **Video** | Screen-record typing the note, then cut to creating the page |
| **Platforms** | X, IG, TikTok, Reddit (as an image in a personal post) |

### 18. Long form — Ship

X thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit post. Video only if you can talk for 60s without pitching features first.

**Thread spine:** I was carrying this goal alone → I made a page → I invited n people → here is what a check-in looks like → link.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Cover image for the thread: `looking-back-moving-forward.png` |
| **Carousel** | LinkedIn version of the same spine |
| **Video** | 60–90s screen-record + voiceover, or talking-head on LinkedIn |
| **Platforms** | X, LinkedIn, Reddit |

### 19. Low-stock alert — Skip

Inventory scare does not apply. Replacement: **offer** as “it’s free, start a page.”

### 20. Meme — Skip / rare Adapt

Only if the template is already about stalled goals, and you add no insult. Default: skip. Replacement: **Venn diagram** or **iPhone Notes**.

### 21. Myth vs fact — Ship

| Myth | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| If I tell people and pause, I’ll look like I quit. | Returning counts. |
| Accountability means a crowd. | A few people is enough. |
| I need a perfect plan first. | A goal needs a direction, not a perfect plan. |
| Sharing is showing off. | Sharing is letting effort be seen. |

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Two blocks, `returning-counts.png` |
| **Carousel** | One myth/fact pair per slide, 4 pairs + CTA |
| **Video** | Flip card, 8s per pair |
| **Platforms** | IG carousel (best), LinkedIn, X |

### 22. Native — Ship (default on X and Reddit)

Looks like a person updating a goal. The URL is the ad.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | OG image of your live goal (auto on X when you paste the link) |
| **Carousel** | Usually skip |
| **Video** | Phone capture of posting a real update |
| **Platforms** | X first, Reddit second |

### 23. New vs old — Adapt

Old = private notes nobody opens. New = the same goal on a page people can see. Do not mock Notion, Strides, or the user’s current tools.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Split: Notes app vs goal page screenshot |
| **Carousel** | Slide 1 old, 2–5 what changes, 6 CTA |
| **Video** | Swipe or split-screen 12s |
| **Platforms** | IG, X, TikTok |

### 24. Offer — Adapt

The offer is: free page, one link, invite who you choose.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | “A public page for a goal. Free.” `share-your-goal.png` |
| **Carousel** | What you get (page, updates, reactions) without fake $ value |
| **Video** | Create-goal flow, 20s, end on the share sheet |
| **Platforms** | All. Strongest as retargeting later, weak as a cold first post. |

### 25. Problem vs solution — Ship

| Problem | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Effort happens in private | A page other people can open |
| Encouragement has nowhere to land | Reactions on an update |
| Restarting feels like starting over | The page is still there |

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Left/right still |
| **Carousel** | Three pairs + CTA |
| **Video** | Cut from a blank notes app to the live page |
| **Platforms** | All |

### 26. Problems — Ship

Name the hole. Product in the caption, not the first line.

**Lines:** The quiet middle. Re-explaining the goal. Nobody to tell when you showed up. A pause that feels like erasure.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | One line + `quiet-middle.png` |
| **Carousel** | One problem per slide, solution only on 5–6 |
| **Video** | Stacked captions, then the page |
| **Platforms** | IG, X, Reels |

### 27. Reasons why — Ship

Use the carousel already written in the production guide (quiet middle). Alternate set: reasons to invite people, not “reasons you’ll succeed.”

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Cover only: “A goal needs a few people. Here’s why.” |
| **Carousel** | Default 6-slide template |
| **Video** | Read slides as 25s Reel |
| **Platforms** | IG, LinkedIn, X (as images) |

### 28. Reddit style — Ship

Write a real post. Screenshot it for X/IG only after it lives on Reddit.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Screenshot of *your* post + comments, or Notes-style body |
| **Carousel** | Skip |
| **Video** | Greenscreen of the thread (later) |
| **Platforms** | Reddit native. X/IG as proof after. |

### 29. Screenshot — Ship

Highest-leverage proof you have. Goal page, one update, a few reactions, share sheet.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Device frame optional. No fake reaction counts. |
| **Carousel** | Create → share → update → reaction |
| **Video** | 20–40s full loop of that path |
| **Platforms** | All. Product Hunt gallery later. |

### 30. Script — Ship (for video)

Talking-head optional. Captions required.

**8-second script**

1. It is hard to keep going when nobody can see the effort.  
2. You don’t need a crowd.  
3. Make a page, invite a few people, post a small check-in.  
4. Who would you want in your corner?

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Line 1 as the still |
| **Carousel** | One beat per slide |
| **Video** | The actual format. Swap line 1 to test. |
| **Platforms** | Reels, TikTok, X |

### 31. Side effect — Adapt

Warm, not medical. “Side effect of sharing a goal: someone texts you on a quiet week.”

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | That line + `encouragement.png` |
| **Carousel** | Three human side effects (a reply, a reminder, a witness) |
| **Video** | Text-message format acting the side effect |
| **Platforms** | IG, X |

### 32. Stat headline — Adapt (sourced only)

Approved if you cite it: Harkin et al., 2016, progress monitoring helps, and public reporting is associated with a larger effect. Matthews 2007 (written goals + weekly accountability) only if you quote the paper honestly and do not imply GoMotivateMe produced that number.

Never invent user counts, completion rates, or “9 out of 10.”

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | One number, one sentence, source in small type |
| **Carousel** | Claim → what the study actually measured → what we built → CTA |
| **Video** | 10s, number on screen first |
| **Platforms** | LinkedIn, X |

### 33. Text message — Ship

Two bubbles. Friend: “How’s the writing?” You: “I posted an update.” Link in caption, not necessarily in the fake thread.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | iMessage on ivory, or overlay on `encouragement.png` *without* covering figures |
| **Carousel** | The thread across 4 slides, CTA on 5 |
| **Video** | Messages appearing on a delay |
| **Platforms** | IG, TikTok, X, Stories |

### 34. Text on skin — Skip

Wrong category. Replacement: **iPhone Notes**.

### 35. Tier list — Adapt

Rank *kinds of support*, not people and not rival apps.

| Rank | Item |
| --- | --- |
| S | Two people who already care |
| A | A public page they can reopen |
| B | A small written check-in |
| C | A private streak only you see |
| D | A motivational quote with no goal attached |

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | The list on ivory, cobalt labels |
| **Carousel** | One tier per slide |
| **Video** | Drag items into rows, 20s |
| **Platforms** | IG, TikTok, X |

### 36. Transformation — Adapt

Process only: unseen → seen. Never body, revenue, or “I hit the goal because of the app” unless that sentence is true and modest.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | `looking-back-moving-forward.png` + “Same goal. Now a few people can see it.” |
| **Carousel** | Week 1 alone → first invite → first reaction → still in progress |
| **Video** | Real dated updates from *your* page |
| **Platforms** | X, IG, LinkedIn |

### 37. Trustpilot reviews — Wait

No store of reviews yet. Replacement: **screenshot** of real reactions on a goal, or **customer testimonial** later.

### 38. Us vs them — Skip as competitor war / Adapt as isolation

Do not roast Habitica, StickK, or “people who use trackers.” Allowed contrast: **doing it unseen** vs **doing it with a few witnesses**. That is problem vs solution, not a tribe.

### 39. Us vs us — Adapt

Evening-you hides the goal. Morning-you wanted company. No “lazy you.”

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Two lines, `quiet-middle.png` |
| **Carousel** | The split → the small action (invite, check-in) → CTA |
| **Video** | Two caption voices, same person |
| **Platforms** | IG, X, Reels |

### 40. Venn diagram — Ship

Ivory, cobalt outlines, no Mastercard parody.

**Circles:** Goals that matter ∩ Goals nobody can see → the quiet middle  
**Or:** People who care about you ∩ People who can actually see the work → your corner

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Best format for this |
| **Carousel** | Build the circles slide by slide |
| **Video** | Circles slide together, 6s |
| **Platforms** | LinkedIn, IG, X |

### 41. Warning — Skip

Replacement: **X signs**. If you need a strong hook, use a calm true line, not a red banner.

### 42. We're sorry — Wait / rare Adapt

Only after a real outage or a real copy mistake. Not “sorry this is free.” Replacement: **native** update.

### 43. Whiteboard — Adapt

Teach the plan: create, share, check in. Readable handwriting or type over `make-a-plan.png`.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | Three steps |
| **Carousel** | Mechanism: why a public URL helps (people don’t have to remember to ask) |
| **Video** | Glass/whiteboard 40s for LinkedIn; or captions-only for Reels |
| **Platforms** | LinkedIn, X |

### 44. X signs — Ship

**You’re in the quiet middle if:** the excitement is gone, you still care, nobody has asked this week, a pause feels heavier than the work.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | “4 signs you’re in the quiet middle” cover |
| **Carousel** | One sign per slide + CTA |
| **Video** | Checklist ticked on screen |
| **Platforms** | IG carousel, Reels, TikTok, X |

### 45. You can avoid — Adapt

Avoidable things: re-explaining the goal from scratch, carrying a pause alone, keeping effort invisible. Do not threaten failure.

| Media | Make this |
| --- | --- |
| **Static** | “You can avoid carrying this one alone.” `people-in-your-corner.png` |
| **Carousel** | Three avoidable frictions + the page |
| **Video** | 12s |
| **Platforms** | IG, X |

### 46. Zero stars — Skip

Fake reviews of “going it alone” still look like fake reviews. Replacement: **problems** or **myth vs fact**.

## Caption formulas

**X:** Hook line. One true detail. Link. One question.  
**IG:** Caption = context the slides don’t hold. First line must work as the preview.  
**TikTok/Reels:** On-screen text is the post. Caption is the CTA plus topic words (`public goal`, `accountability`, `quiet middle`).  
**LinkedIn:** Scene → lesson → product as the modest tool → CTA.  
**Reddit:** Story first. Tool once, in context. No CTA buttons.

---

## Pre-publish check

- Is the person with the goal the hero?
- Is there one CTA?
- Did you invent a result, a user, or a review?
- Would this still be true if they pause for a month?
- Static: one image, one idea, approved art or a real screenshot?
- Carousel: six jobs, six slides, no collage?
- Video: hook in one second, captions on, no logo sting?
