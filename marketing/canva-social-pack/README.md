## Canva use

1. Upload the original PNG; do not add text or logos to the artwork itself.
2. For a square post, leave the image uncropped and place the headline in the clear upper half.
3. For Stories or Reels, start with an ivory `#FBFAF6` 1080 × 1920 canvas, place the square artwork in the lower half, and use the upper half for the headline and a short supporting line.
4. Use cobalt `#2856C7` for copy and yellow `#FEB704` sparingly as an accent. Keep layouts calm and text-led rather than adding decorative elements.
5. Add one primary CTA only, such as “Share your goal,” “Invite your people,” or “Start your page.”

Use the story-first rules in [the LLM media guide](../story-first-llm-media-guide.md): the audience is the hero and GoMotivateMe is the guide. Avoid guaranteed outcomes, productivity-shame, visual clutter, and generic feature announcements.

For repeatable multi-slide posts, follow the [carousel production guide](../carousel-production-guide.md).

## Visual system

The pack follows the [journey illustration guide](../../public/illustrations/journey/README.md): warm ivory paper, cobalt monoprint, one golden-yellow sun, small human figures, tactile print texture, and no in-image text, UI, symbols, gradients, or photography.
